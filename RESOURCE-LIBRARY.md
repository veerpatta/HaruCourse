# Resource library

Canonical researched catalog. Review date: **2026-09-06**. The entire 17-area map has a primary and an alternative; this is a researched starting path, not a claim that every advanced subtopic or tool workflow is finished. See the explicit gaps below before authoring.

## Verification and selection rules

**Verified** means the specified public reading was retrieved and its teaching scope reviewed on the stated date. It does not certify a SaaS workflow. **Candidate** means further access, scope or exercise testing is needed; it cannot be the sole required path. **Unavailable** means this review could not retrieve it, not that the site is permanently offline.

Every R01–R28 row below has: status **verified (reading)**; verification date **2026-09-06**; format **HTML text with examples/illustrations** (R24 is an online book); account **none for required reading**; price **free reading, no card or trial**. The successful public retrieval of the exact linked page is the access evidence. Paid training, promoted products and optional purchases on those sites are excluded. Effort is a course-author estimate in minutes for the named selection, not an imposed limit or publisher claim. Text is preferred for searching, low bandwidth and pausing; evaluate diagrams and accessibility in the authored exercise. No copyright permission to copy full pages is implied.

## Supporting programs and placement

**Tools (m03, m08–m16):** Essential capabilities are layout, components, prototyping, documentation and evidence handling, not a long list of subscriptions. Figma is important for industry familiarity (T01, validate Starter before a required tool-specific lesson). FigJam, Miro and Notion are useful optional collaboration interfaces; paper maps and local Markdown are the required equivalents. Illustrator/Photoshop are optional visual-production references; use existing skills, SVG in a text editor and original assets without subscription requirements. After Effects/ProtoPie are optional motion references; use R13 CSS or a frame storyboard. Framer/Webflow are optional publishing references; the required responsive project is local HTML/CSS using R15/R16, with hosting optional. Jira/Confluence are useful delivery vocabulary; R18/R26 and local Markdown provide the exercise. Analytics/behavior tools are useful only when ethically collected evidence exists; R20 is required, T03 optional. AI assistance is optional and may use the learner's existing free access; scripted examples support R21 without API spending. Each future tool lesson must name the target skill, resource, exercise, exported artifact, limitations and mini-project contribution.

**Portfolio (m05–m19):** Project 1 is a research-led everyday service (m05–m11); project 2 is a responsive commerce or information product (m12–m15); project 3 is a complex B2B/service/AI workflow (m16–m18). Select domains based on real participant access. Each includes problem/context/users/business, research and competition, synthesis/JTBD, journey, IA/flows, wireframes, prototype/UI/system, tests, iterations, final solution, honest metrics, reflection and case study. No invented interviews or impact. Optional fourth/fifth projects only address a demonstrated portfolio gap. R22/R23 guide presentation.

**Books:** Must learn the concepts, not purchase a book. R24 is the highly recommended legal free online book (one chapter and applied reflection per chosen session). R01/R02 are the free foundational reading path. Advanced reading is selected R21 chapters; reference reading is R14/R15/R17. Paid titles are not required; add optional paid references only with an approved free concept alternative and publisher link checked at authoring. Do not link pirated PDFs.

**Career (m20):** Use R23 to build a competency matrix and R22 to prepare a portfolio walkthrough; draft resume, LinkedIn summary, truthful career-transition story and outreach locally. Practice portfolio presentation, critique, whiteboard challenge, behavioral examples and take-home scope negotiation. Before teaching market conclusions, gather dated India and India-eligible remote vacancies from multiple employers, compare junior/mid expectations, location and skills; vacancies are evidence, not promised opportunities. No outreach is sent automatically.

**Current awareness (all modules, optional):** At a chosen study session spend 10–15 minutes on R25 or an official tool change log. At a module review compare one change to a stable standard. At a portfolio checkpoint decide whether to adopt it. Log source/date, evidence, applicability and a tiny test. Missing these check-ins never loses progress. Blogs, communities and social posts may suggest questions, but primary evidence decides recommendations.
