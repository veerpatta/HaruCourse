## 2. Arithmetic and fact checks

- l02 check 1: 10 min producing + 20 min reading/discarding/editing = 30 min, against 40 min by hand → about 10 min saved (with the stated quality caveat).
- l01 supported: WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3 (AA) requires 4.5:1 for normal-size text (3:1 large, as R30's catalog row states); SC 1.4.6 (AAA) requires 7:1. 5.2:1 ≥ 4.5:1 passes AA and < 7:1 misses AAA, so the supplied output states the wrong level as the minimum. The 7:1 AAA figure is from the standard as I know it and was not re-fetched this session.
- l09 supported: the 62% match rate and unsourced "40% benchmark" are presented as claims to reject, not facts.
- No other figure in the module was changed; "62 per cent" and "one in ten" remain labelled made-up example data.
