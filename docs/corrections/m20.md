## 2. Arithmetic and fact checks

- **Tally:** 11 calls in 5 working days is 2.2 a day; 7 in 5 is 1.4 a day. "Eleven a day" over five days would be 55 calls, so a per-day wording multiplies the claim by five. The fall is 4/11 = 36.4%, which matches the distractors' "36 per cent" and "more than a third".
- **Owner's estimate:** 6–10 a day is 30–50 per five days. That is 2.7–4.5 times the tally (30/11, 50/11), so the estimate is kept apart from the count.
- **Percentages:** 11/14 = 0.786, which rounds to 79%. 9/14 = 64.3%.
- **Fit counts:** eligibility 2 + 1 + 11 = 14; location 6 + 5 + 3 = 14; hours 4 + 10 = 14.
- **Capture (l02):** 26 − 14 = 12 failures = 7 + 3 + 2. Transfer task: 12 − 3 − 1 = 8 captured.
- **Course record (l01):** 1 + 1 + 6 + 1 = 9 outcomes for 8 URLs, which `RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md` does not reconcile.
- **Outreach (l09):** 2 replied + 1 bounced + 3 silent = 6; 3 replies in the end.
- **Time zones:**
  - 9:00–13:00 US Eastern is 19:30–23:30 IST on standard time (UTC−5 vs UTC+5:30, a 10:30 difference) and 18:30–22:30 IST on summer time (9:30 difference).
  - CET 9:00–17:00 is 13:30–21:30 IST in winter, so a 10:00–19:00 IST day overlaps 13:30–19:00 (5.5 hours, at least the 4 required).
- **Transfer tasks:** 250 → 350 = +40%; 12 → 6 = half; 15/20 = 75%.
