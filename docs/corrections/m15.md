Wilson 95%: centre (p + z²/2n)/(1 + z²/n), half-width z·√(p(1−p)/n + z²/4n²)/(1 + z²/n).
- 9/22 = 40.9% → 23.3% to 61.3% (text previously said 24 to 61; corrected to 23).

Newcombe (method 10) for p1 − p2: lower = d − √((p1−l1)² + (u2−p2)²), upper = d + √((u1−p1)² + (p2−l2)²).
- 44/100 vs 36/100: d = 8.0 points, −5.50 to +21.10 (separate intervals 34.7–53.8 and 27.3–45.8 overlap).
